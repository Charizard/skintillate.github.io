meet the doctor

dr. kiruthika

Dr. Kiruthika is a distinguished Dermatologist with over 10 years of experience in the field of Dermatology, combining academic excellence, clinical expertise, research, and a passion for aesthetic medicine. She completed her MBBS from Madras Medical College, Chennai, followed by MD in Dermatology, Venereology and Leprology from the prestigious PGIMER, Chandigarh, and DNB in Dermatology, Venereology and Leprology from the National Board of Examinations, New Delhi. She has served as a Senior Resident at both JIPMER, Puducherry and PGIMER, Chandigarh, gaining extensive experience in managing diverse dermatological conditions. Her academic and research interests include dermatologic surgeries and advanced aesthetic procedures, with her research work receiving prestigious medals at national conferences. She has also participated as faculty at multiple national and international conferences and has authored research publications and textbook chapters. With a commitment to evidence-based, personalized care, Dr. Kiruthika strives to bring together precision in treatment, artistry in aesthetics, and compassion in patient care—helping every individual achieve healthier skin and renewed confidence.
